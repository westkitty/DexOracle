# Audio Manifest

All song stage mixes were rendered locally from the uploaded `Happy Mother's Day! Stems (102BPM).zip`.

- Source stem sample rate: 48000 Hz
- Loop duration: 57.740354 seconds
- Browser playback uses OGG first and MP3 fallback.
- Beach ambience and small interaction sounds are procedural/generated for this package, keeping redistribution clean.

## Music stages

- `music-stage-0.ogg/.mp3`: synth
- `music-stage-1.ogg/.mp3`: synth, percussion
- `music-stage-2.ogg/.mp3`: synth, percussion, bass
- `music-stage-3.ogg/.mp3`: synth, percussion, bass, drums
- `music-stage-4.ogg/.mp3`: synth, percussion, bass, drums, guitar
- `music-stage-5.ogg/.mp3`: synth, percussion, bass, drums, guitar, backing vocals
- `music-stage-6.ogg/.mp3`: synth, percussion, bass, drums, guitar, backing vocals, lead vocals
- `music-stage-7.ogg/.mp3`: synth, percussion, bass, drums, guitar, backing vocals, lead vocals, other
